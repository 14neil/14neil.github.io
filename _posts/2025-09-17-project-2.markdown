description: "One useful method for probing our own Galaxy is by detecting the radio wave emission that the Galaxy emits. With help from the Leuschner Observatory 4.5 radio dish (LEFT), measuring the brightness temperatures throughout the Galactic disk can allow us to probe the structure of the Galaxy. The RIGHT image is a modeled heat map of the Galaxy where Galactic topocentric coordinates are mapped on the x-axis and y-axis. The colors highlight various observed brightness temperatures and the white region represents unobserved Galactic coordinates. The heat map can be used to estimate the masses of the observed Galactic spiral arms."
